# Prompt Library for PMs & PgMs

High-quality, ready-to-use prompts. Copy and paste into any AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, etc.).

---

## First 90 Days Onboarding

**1. Stakeholder Introduction Email**  
"Write a professional but warm 1:1 introduction email to a key stakeholder. I am the new [Your Role] on the [Program Name] program. The program goal is [brief goal]."

**2. Team Shadowing Analysis**  
"Analyze these observations from a team meeting I shadowed and give me actionable insights: [Paste your observations]"

**3. Project History Audit**  
"Help me identify risks, gaps, and themes from this project history: [Paste key points from docs]"

**4. Dependency Mapping**  
"Help me build a comprehensive dependency map. Here is what I know so far: [Paste known dependencies and teams]"

**5. Executive Sponsor Communication Plan**  
"Create a communication plan tailored for my executive sponsor. They prefer [email/Slack/verbal] updates. Key information: [List key points]"

---

## Communication & Stakeholder Management

**6. BLUF Executive Update**  
"Write a concise BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) executive update on this topic: [Describe current status]"

**7. Difficult Conversation Prep**  
"Help me prepare for a difficult conversation with a stakeholder. Context: [Describe the situation and desired outcome]"

**8. Escalation Email**  
"Draft a clear, professional escalation email for this blocker: [Describe the issue, impact, and what I need]"

**9. RACI Alignment Workshop**  
"Create an agenda and facilitation guide for a RACI alignment workshop with cross-functional teams."

**10. Stakeholder Feedback Request**  
"Write a professional email asking a stakeholder for honest feedback on my performance so far."

---

## Status Reporting & Meetings

**11. Weekly Status Report**  
"Write a professional weekly status report. Wins: [list]. Risks: [list]. Next steps: [list]."

**12. Meeting Agenda Creator**  
"Create a focused and effective meeting agenda for a [Meeting Type] with these goals: [List goals]"

**13. Retrospective Questions**  
"Generate 10 strong retrospective questions for a program-level retrospective."

**14. Status Email Template**  
"Create a concise status email for [Audience] about this situation: [Describe situation]"

---

## Risk, Decisions & Leadership

**15. Risk Register Entry**  
"Turn this into a professional risk register entry with probability, impact, owner, and mitigation: [Describe risk]"

**16. Decision Record (DACI)**  
"Create a Decision Record using the DACI framework for this decision: [Describe the decision]"

**17. Prioritization Framework**  
"Help me prioritize these items using MoSCoW method and business value scoring: [List items]"

**18. Lessons Learned Summary**  
"Help me document key lessons learned from this experience: [Describe what happened]"

**19. Trade-off Analysis**  
"Perform a trade-off analysis between these two options: [Option A] vs [Option B]"

**20. One-on-One Agenda with Direct Report**  
"Create a productive 1:1 agenda template for a direct report who is [describe situation]"

## Bonus Advanced Prompts

**21. Program Health Assessment**  
"Act as a senior TPM and give me an honest assessment of program health based on this information: [Paste details]"

**22. Executive Presentation Outline**  
"Create a compelling executive presentation outline for this program update: [Key points]"

**23. Change Impact Assessment**  
"Help me assess the impact of this change across people, process, and technology: [Describe change]"

**24. Negotiation Preparation**  
"Prepare me for a negotiation with [Team/Vendor] on [topic]. My goals are: [List goals]"

**25. Personal Branding as TPM**  
"Help me craft my narrative as a Technical Program Manager for [LinkedIn / performance review / intro deck]"

**Tip:** Replace anything in [brackets] with your specific context for the best results.
