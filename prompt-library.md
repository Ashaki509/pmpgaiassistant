# Prompt Library for PMs & PgMs

Copy and paste these prompts into any AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, etc.).

---

## First 90 Days Onboarding

**1. Stakeholder Introduction Email**
"Write a professional but warm 1:1 intro email to a key stakeholder on a new program. My role is [Your Role]. The program is [Brief Description]."

**2. Shadowing Observation Summary**
"Turn these observations from a team meeting into actionable insights: [Paste what you observed]"

**3. Dependency Mapping Prompt**
"Help me map dependencies for this project. Here is what I know so far: [Paste info]"

**4. Executive Sponsor Communication**
"Create a status update for my executive sponsor. Key points: [List key points]. They prefer [email/Slack/verbal] updates."

---

## Communication & Stakeholder Management

**5. BLUF Executive Update**
"Write a BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) executive update about this situation: [Describe situation]"

**6. Difficult Stakeholder Conversation**
"Help me prepare for a conversation with a resistant stakeholder. Context: [Describe context]"

**7. Escalation Email**
"Draft a clear escalation email regarding this blocker: [Describe blocker]"

**8. RACI Workshop Facilitation**
"Create an agenda and talking points for a RACI alignment workshop with cross-functional teams."

---

## Status Reporting & Meetings

**9. Weekly Status Report**
"Write a concise weekly status report. Highlights: [List wins]. Risks: [List risks]."

**10. Retrospective Facilitation**
"Generate 8 good retrospective questions for a program-level retro."

---

## Risk & Decision Making

**11. Risk Register Entry**
"Turn this risk into a professional risk register entry with probability, impact, and mitigation: [Describe risk]"

**12. Decision Document**
"Create a Decision Record (DACI) for this decision: [Describe decision]"

---

## General Useful Prompts

**13. Meeting Agenda Creator**
"Create a focused meeting agenda for [Meeting Type] with these goals: [List goals]"

**14. Lessons Learned**
"Help me document lessons learned from this experience: [Describe what happened]"

**15. Prioritization Help**
"Help me prioritize these 12 items using MoSCoW + business value scoring: [List items]"
