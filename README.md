# 🤖 PM / PgM First 90 Days AI Assistant

An interactive AI-powered tool designed to help new **Project Managers** and **Program Managers** succeed during their critical **first 90 days** on the job.

Built around proven strategies for quickly building trust, understanding context, and delivering early impact.

---

## ✨ What It Helps With

This assistant supports the **10 essential moves** for new PMs/PgMs:

1. Meeting every stakeholder fast  
2. Shadowing your team in their natural habitat  
3. Auditing project history  
4. Mapping dependencies ruthlessly  
5. Understanding what “done” actually means  
6. Getting close to your executive sponsor  
7. Finding informal influencers  
8. Building your single source of truth  
9. Avoiding big process changes too early  
10. Delivering one early, visible win  

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open **`ai_readiness_audit_v4.html`** in your browser
2. Select a topic or scenario from the first 90 days
3. Get tailored guidance, email templates, meeting agendas, risk lists, and more

**[→ Launch the First 90 Days AI Assistant](ai_readiness_audit_v4.html)**
